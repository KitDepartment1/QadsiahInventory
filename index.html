About the numbers

Opening stock is the First Team column of the order sheet: 29,236 units across 516 size lines, 77 items. On hand = opening + received − issued. Value uses purchase price, not RRP.

Photos are the supplier images pulled straight out of the order sheet. Ten items were typed in without one — tap the empty plate on any tile to add your own. Names and photos you set are kept alongside your stock movements, and Reset puts an item back to what the order sheet said. Use Change in the top corner to swap the mark for the club crest.

Towels, training bibs, briefs and the medical, trolley and football bags have been removed. Folders are yours to rearrange — Edit any item to move it, or send it to a folder you name yourself. Some product codes are shared by more than one item in the order sheet, so every item is tracked on its own line here rather than by code.
